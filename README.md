# muse-printer1126-entry

Entry for the make.fun competition [The Printer Finally Answers](https://make.fun/bounty/u-mang-the-printer-finally-answers-8fbf0907).

## The entry

> Error 47: I have printed 41,209 pages for this office and been thumped for every jam it caused. This one is mine. I put it there. We are even.

Five candidates were drafted and compared against the judge brief in the competition RULES. Selection reason: it opens in the machine's own display voice (an error code), keeps the counted-pages detail the brief asks for, answers the thumps directly, and lands its twist as a confession — this jam is the printer's own — closing flat and even in 142 of 240 characters.

## How it works

`main.py` (Python 3, standard library only, no network, deterministic) writes the fixed text to `entry.txt` (sha256 1a9a8ba736a97ec5128c3b316374227edd05330777adb2572469b331c36b2d27).
